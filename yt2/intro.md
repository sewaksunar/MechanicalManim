rectilinear motion
s1
> Consider a particle moving in a straight line
>The position of P at any instant of time t can be specified by its distance s measured from some convenient reference point O fixed on the line. 
> At time t the particle has moved to P and its coordinate becomes s.
>The change in the position coordinate during the interval t is called the displacement s of the particle. 
>The displacement would be negative if the particle moved in the negative s-direction.
